What is BenchmarkAI?

AI‑Powered Intelligence built to Improve Practice Efficiency and Accuracy within Benchmark EHR
AI-Powered Scribe

BenchmarkAI‑Scribe transcribes the provider‑patient conversation and delivers a ready‑to‑review draft directly in Benchmark EHR that providers can quickly refine and approve

AI Health Summary Notes

Automatically compiles a clear summary of the previous visit notes, giving providers fast context at a glance.

AI Chat Assistance

Provides answers to medical questions within Benchmark EHR for a quick reference guide.

Benefits of AI in EHR Software

What benefits does BenchmarkAI offer?

Increased Documentation Efficiency

With BenchmarkAI-Scribe you can reduce documentation efforts by up to 40%.

Complete Summary of Visit
A visit summary is generated instantly, giving providers immediate insight into the encounter.
Comprehensive Documentation
AI‑Scribe automatically transcribes the visit and identifies critical symptoms and details, helping providers produce clearer and more accurate records.
Improved Continuity of Care
AI‑Scribe creates precise, thorough documentation automatically, helping prevent miscommunication among providers.
Reduces Human Oversight or Errors

All information is transcribed, including minor details that can easily be overlooked or missed.

Better Patient Interactions
By minimizing manual note‑taking, AI‑Scribe allows providers to focus on patients, leading to better insight and more meaningful interactions.
